Burj Khalifa

The Burj Khalifa[a] (previously known as Burj Dubai prior to inauguration) is a megatall skyscraper in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, it is the world's tallest structure, with a total height of 829.8 m (2,722 ft, or just over half a mile) and a roof height (excluding the antenna, but including a 242.6 m spire) of 828 m (2,717 ft). It has also been the tallest building in the world since its topping out in 2009, surpassing Taipei 101, which had held the record since 2004.

Shanghai Tower

The Shanghai Tower is a 128-story, 632 m (2,073 ft) megatall skyscraper in Lujiazui, Pudong, Shanghai. It is the tallest building in China and the second-tallest building in the world after the Burj Khalifa in Dubai. The tower was designed by Gensler, an American architectural firm, and developed by Shanghai Tower Construction and Development. Construction of the tower began in November 2008 and was completed in September 2015.

Abraj Al-Bait Clock Tower

The Abraj Al-Bait Clock Tower, also known as the Makkah Royal Clock Tower, is a government-owned complex of seven skyscraper hotels in Mecca, Saudi Arabia. The central hotel tower, which is the tallest of the seven, is the third-tallest building and fifth-tallest freestanding structure in the world. It is also the tallest clock tower in the world. The complex is part of the King Abdulaziz Endowment Project that aims to modernize the city in catering to its pilgrims.

Ping An Finance Center

The Ping An Finance Center is a 115-story, 599 m (1,965 ft) supertall skyscraper in Shenzhen, Guangdong. It is the second-tallest building in China and the fourth-tallest building in the world. The tower was designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates and developed by Ping An Insurance. Construction of the tower began in 2010 and was completed in 2017.

Lotte World Tower

The Lotte World Tower is a 123-story, 555 m (1,819 ft) supertall skyscraper in Seoul, South Korea. It is the tallest building in South Korea and the fifth-tallest building in the world. The tower was designed by Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates and developed by Lotte Corporation. Construction of the tower began in 2011 and was completed in 2016.
